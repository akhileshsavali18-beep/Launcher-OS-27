Privacy Policy — Launcher OS 27

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Launcher OS 27 is an Android launcher application. This policy explains how the app may access device information needed to provide launcher features.

Information and permissions

The app may access installed application information to display and organize apps. Location may be requested for weather information. Notification access is optional and is used only to display notifications inside the launcher. Bluetooth, camera, flashlight, battery and device settings may be accessed only when required by the corresponding feature and Android permissions.

Advertising and purchases

The app may use Google Mobile Ads and Google Play Billing for advertising and optional premium features. Their handling of data is governed by the applicable Google policies and privacy terms.

Data storage

Launcher preferences and notification history may be stored locally on the device. We do not intentionally sell personal information.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support page.
