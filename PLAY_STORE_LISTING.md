## Full description
Launcher OS 27 is a customizable Android launcher designed for a clean, modern home-screen experience.

### Features
- Customizable home screen and dock
- App folders with rename and organization
- Advanced App Library with search, categories, recent apps and A–Z navigation
- Clock, weather, battery, calendar and recent-app widgets
- Dynamic Island for music, timers, charging, Bluetooth and call states
- Notification Center with notification history and app launching
- Control Center with Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, flashlight, airplane mode, brightness and volume controls
- Battery & Device dashboard
- Weather powered by Open-Meteo when location access is granted
- Lock Screen overlay with clock, weather, battery, flashlight and camera shortcuts
- Premium option and Google Play Billing architecture
- Ad-supported free experience

### Permissions
Some features are optional and require Android permissions or user-enabled system access. These include location for weather, calendar for calendar events, Bluetooth access for supported device status, and notification access for Notification Center.

### Compatibility
Android 8.0 (API 26) and newer, subject to device capabilities.
